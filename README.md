# My Favorite Movie: Fast & Furious

## About the Movie

Fast & Furious is one of my favorite action movie series. It is about street racing, heists, and a crew led by Dominic Toretto who protect each other like family.

## Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because:

- It has exciting car chases and stunts.
- It shows the value of loyalty and family.
- It has memorable characters.
- It is fun and entertaining to watch.

## My Favorite Characters

1. Dominic Toretto
2. Brian O'Conner
3. Letty Ortiz
4. Roman Pearce
5. Tej Parker

## My Favorite Quote

> I don't have friends, I got family.

This quote reminds me how important family is.
